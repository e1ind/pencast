# PenCast demo deck

`PenCast_Demo.pdf`: 14 slides (plus an appendix), one feature each, for recording a demo.
Rebuild with `./build.sh` (renders the videos and animation frames with numpy/matplotlib/ffmpeg,
then compiles with LuaLaTeX). The videos have no audio, so your narration stays clean.

## Shot list

| # | Slide | What to do on camera |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Open the deck from the home screen. |
| 2 | One PDF. No conversion. | Just present; this is the pitch. |
| 3 | Embedded videos play in place | Let the reaction–diffusion video autoplay. If a projector is connected, show both screens. |
| 4 | Write right over a playing video | Fountain pen: circle a few bright fringes while the waves move. |
| 5 | Tap a video to control it | Tap the vortex video: play, pause, drag the scrubber, and let the bar fade away. |
| 6 | animate sequences | Let the epicycles run, then tap to pause and tap again to resume. |
| 7 | Notes survive every `\pause` | Underline the first line, then advance three times: the ink stays as each line appears. |
| 8 | Point with the laser | Laser with trail: trace the envelope, lift and let it fade. Switch to *Dot only* and point at the period label. |
| 9 | Zoom in to write neatly | Pinch on the table, drag with one finger, and circle the one decimal number (9.07). Two-finger tap to zoom out. |
| 10 | Fountain pen | Open the pen menu (tap the pen twice), show the pressure sliders and the test pad, then work the example on the grid (λ = 1, 3). Use the lasso to move a line. |
| 11 | Links | Tap *Jump to the appendix*, then *Back*. |
| 12 | Made for the room | Show the projector menu (black / freeze), the timer, and *Projector Follows Zoom*. |
| 13 | Take your notes with you | Share icon → *Notes* → *Share…* (or *Save As…*); show the PDF with one page per slide and your ink. |
| 14 | Closing | Title card for the end of the video. |

Tip: for the App Store, record the iPad screen with Control Center → Screen Recording; for a two-screen shot,
mirror to a display over AirPlay or HDMI and film or capture it too.
